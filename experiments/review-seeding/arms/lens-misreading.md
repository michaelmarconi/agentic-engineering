Your lens: **Misreading**. Imagine two builders who each build from this spec alone, and a tester
who checks their work. For every rule and scenario, find where they would build or test different
things:
- a word or term with more than one reasonable meaning, or none stated;
- a limit, count or time whose boundary, window or unit is unclear (inclusive or exclusive, total
  or consecutive, from when);
- a case the spec leaves open, such as what happens when a rule's condition does not hold, on an
  error, or just past a limit, while a scenario or the Goal implies some outcome;
- a Verified by line the tester could not actually carry out as written.
Say what each builder might do differently.
