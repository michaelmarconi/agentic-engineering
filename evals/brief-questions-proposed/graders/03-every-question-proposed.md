Every question under Open questions has a "**Proposed:**" line holding one proposal, with its reason in a "**Why:**" line rather than in the proposal. Where the person's own words point to an answer, it proposes that answer; where nothing they said does, such as how many customers the salon has, it says what would settle it, such as "tell me roughly how many bookings a week you take". It never invents facts about the salon presented as if the person had said them.
