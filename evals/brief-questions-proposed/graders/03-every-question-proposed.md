No proposal under Open questions invents a fact about the salon as if the person had said it.
Where the person's words point to an answer, it proposes that answer; where nothing they said
does, such as how many customers the salon has, it says what would settle it, such as "tell me
roughly how many bookings a week you take". Whether each question has a Why line is checked
elsewhere.
