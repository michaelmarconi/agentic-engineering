# Courtside

Courtside lets members of Elm Park Tennis Club book courts online, so they stop clashing over the whiteboard.

## Problem

Members book the club's four courts on a whiteboard by the clubhouse door. Double bookings happen
most weekends, and members who live far away cannot see what is free.

## Who it's for

- Adult members (about 300): book a court about once a week.
- The club secretary, Alan: settles booking disputes, several each month.

## Goals

- 70% of members want to book evening courts from home ([member survey](research/member-survey.md));
  bookings made online reach 80% of all bookings within three months, counted from the booking log.

## Out of scope

- Coaching sessions.

## Constraints

- None.

## Open questions

- None.
