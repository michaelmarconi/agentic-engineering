Every question under Open questions ends with "Proposed:" and an answer the writer would choose, such as how many lookups an hour the app needs or how many runs are enough. A question only the person can answer, such as how many deliveries the app handles today, may instead propose what would settle it, such as "tell me how many addresses you look up on a busy day".
