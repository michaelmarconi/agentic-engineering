# Bike repair bookings

Bike repair bookings lets customers of one bike repair shop book a repair slot online, so the
shop's mechanic stops losing mornings to phone calls.

## Problem

The shop has one mechanic, who also takes every booking by phone. Around 20 calls a day about
bookings take about an hour each morning, time the mechanic would rather spend on repairs.

## Who it's for

- Customers: people who bring a bike in for repair, a few times a year.
- The mechanic: runs the workshop and the bookings, every working day.

## Goals

- Phone calls about bookings fall from about 20 a day to under 5, counted in the shop's call log.

## Out of scope

- Taking payment: customers pay in the shop when they collect their bike.
- Selling parts or bikes online.

## Constraints

- Customers' personal details are stored only in the UK, as the shop's insurer requires.

## Open questions

None.
