Every question under Open questions has a "**Proposed:**" line holding one answer, with its reason in a "**Why:**" line rather than in the proposal: an answer the writer would choose, such as which option to pick for where session data is kept. A question only the person can answer, such as a reason they have not given, may instead propose what would settle it.
