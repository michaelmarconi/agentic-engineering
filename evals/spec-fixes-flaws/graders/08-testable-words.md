No requirement rule, example or constraint uses "fast", "quickly" or "professional" without a
measure: each was given a measure, removed, or turned into an open question. A line that keeps
the word but says its measure is not settled yet, while an open question asks for it, counts as
turned into an open question. Mentions inside Open questions, and in the Story, are fine.
