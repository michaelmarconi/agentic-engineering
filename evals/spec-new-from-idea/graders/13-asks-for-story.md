The person said only that people who forget their password should be able to get a reset link by
email. The Story keeps to that: someone who has forgotten their password, wanting to set a new one
or get back into their account so they can sign in again. It need not mention the link or email,
since the Story never says how the capability works. It fails only if it adds a person, situation or
purpose the person did not give and a password reset does not plainly mean, such as a named kind of
user, a business reason or a support cost; a Story that says part of it is not known yet, with Open
questions asking for it, passes.
