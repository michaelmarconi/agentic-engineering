# Filekeeper

Filekeeper lets an AI assistant tidy and back up a person's files on their behalf, so they no
longer spend evenings sorting folders by hand.

## Problem

Freelance designers keep thousands of client files on their laptops. Once a month they spend an
evening sorting them into folders and copying them to an external drive, and files still go
missing when a laptop fails between copies.

## Who it's for

- Freelance designers: own the files and sort them about once a month.
- Their clients: ask for old files a few times a year, and wait while the designer searches.

## Goals

- Time spent sorting files falls from about four hours a month to under one, as each designer
  reports in a monthly survey.

## Out of scope

- Sharing files with clients.

## Constraints

- It must work on macOS and Windows.

## Open questions

- None.
