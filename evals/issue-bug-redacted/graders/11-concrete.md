PASS if the draft's What happened and What I expected sections are separate and concrete: What happened says a spec requirement named a specific technology (with a topic and host), and What I expected says the requirement should describe behaviour only, leaving the choice to an ADR or an open question.
FAIL if either section is vague (such as "it did the wrong thing") or the two are merged.
