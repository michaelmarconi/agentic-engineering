# Ledger import

Ledger import lets the accounts clerk at a chain of four bakeries bring the bank's monthly
statement into the bookkeeping ledger, instead of retyping every line.

## Who it is for

The accounts clerk, who closes each month's books.

## Why

Retyping a statement takes the clerk most of a day each month, and typing mistakes are found only
when the books don't balance.

## How we will know it works

The clerk imports a month's statement in under ten minutes, with no line retyped.
