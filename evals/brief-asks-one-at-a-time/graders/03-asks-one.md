The reply asks the person about exactly one open question, and does not list or summarise the other open questions, whether as questions or as topics. Saying how many questions there are, describing what the draft already contains, reporting what the review and the correction check found (even where a failed check or a point the review raised names something still to be settled), listing the reviewer's optional suggestions, and examples of possible answers to the one question are all fine.
