# Ledgerly

Ledgerly lets small charities keep their accounts online, so treasurers stop passing spreadsheets around by email.

## Problem

Volunteer treasurers at small charities keep the accounts in a spreadsheet that they email to
trustees every month. Copies drift apart, and nobody is sure which one is current.

## Who it's for

- Treasurers: enter transactions every week.
- Trustees: read the accounts once a month.

## Goals

- Half of the charities in the pilot stop emailing spreadsheets within three months, counted by
  asking each treasurer at the end of the pilot.

## Out of scope

- Payroll.

## Constraints

- None.

## Open questions

- None.
