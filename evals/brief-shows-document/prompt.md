Here's the conversation we had this morning about our shift-swap app, pasted in:

> **Me:** Nurses on Ward 7 swap shifts by posting in a group chat and writing it on a paper sheet in the staff room. Dana, the ward manager, spends about three hours a week working out who is actually on shift.
> **You:** Who would use it?
> **Me:** The ward's 40 nurses, who each swap about twice a month, and Dana, who approves every swap.
> **You:** How will you know it's working?
> **Me:** Dana's reconciling time drops below 30 minutes a week. She logs her admin time already.
> **Me:** We'll build it in Flutter, by the way.
> **Me:** Oh, and a swap request should expire after 48 hours if nobody takes it.
> **You:** Anything it won't do?
> **Me:** It won't do payroll, and it won't plan the whole rota.
> **Me:** Staff records have to stay in the UK, that's hospital policy.
> **Me:** Actually, 30 minutes is unrealistic. Say under an hour a week.
> **You:** Should agency nurses use it too?
> **Me:** Not sure yet.
